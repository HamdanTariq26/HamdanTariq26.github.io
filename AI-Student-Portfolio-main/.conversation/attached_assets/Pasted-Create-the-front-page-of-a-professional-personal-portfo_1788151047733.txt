Create the **front page of a professional personal portfolio website** for an undergraduate Artificial Intelligence student.

The portfolio will be viewed by **professors, researchers, recruiters, software/AI companies, collaborators, and other technical professionals**, so the website should feel polished, credible, modern, and high-quality rather than like a generic student template.

The person behind the portfolio is an **Artificial Intelligence undergraduate at NUST (Balochistan Campus)** with a strong interest in building practical intelligent systems. His areas of interest include:

* Artificial Intelligence
* Machine Learning
* Computer Vision
* Robotics
* Autonomous Systems

The homepage should immediately communicate **who he is, what he does, what he is interested in, and why his work is worth exploring**.

Design and structure the page with strong visual hierarchy and excellent UX. The hero section should contain his name, a concise professional introduction, and clear calls to action leading visitors toward his projects, about section, or contact information.

The page should make his technical identity and engineering mindset clear without becoming overloaded with information. It should feel like the portfolio of someone who is **serious about AI, enjoys building real systems, and is progressing toward professional and research-oriented work**.

Use concise, professional copy and avoid exaggerated claims, unnecessary buzzwords, cliché phrases, or overly decorative elements.

The homepage should also include enough supporting content to give visitors a quick understanding of his work and interests while encouraging them to explore the rest of the portfolio.

Make the implementation **fully responsive**, accessible, fast, and production-quality. Pay attention to spacing, typography, component consistency, interactions, responsive behavior, and overall polish.

The final result should feel **distinctive, professional, technically credible, and suitable for an AI student's professional portfolio**, rather than resembling a generic portfolio template.
